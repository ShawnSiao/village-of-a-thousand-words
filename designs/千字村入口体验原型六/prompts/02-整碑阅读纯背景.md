# 整碑阅读纯背景

用途：「开村旧碑」连续阅读页面的氛围背景。

```text
Use case: precise-object-edit
Asset type: Chinese narrative game full-stele reading background
Input images: Image 1 is the edit target and visual-style reference
Primary request: preserve the rainy ancestral hall, carved dark stone, candlelight, aged materials and solemn visual style. Recompose the scene so a tall complete stone stele stands on the left 30% and the right 70% becomes quiet dark architectural negative space for a large front-end parchment reader.
Composition/framing: wide landscape game screen; left third contains the full readable silhouette of the stele and a small warm candle; right two-thirds contain only subdued dark wooden wall and soft shadow, with no built-in panels. The center must not compete with a real HTML reading sheet.
Lighting/mood: dark charcoal hall, muted bronze and candle amber, strong but gentle separation for a high-contrast paper overlay.
Constraints: no parchment panels, no blank cards, no interface frames, no labels, no letters, no Chinese characters, no numbers, no stamps, no buttons, no ruled lines and no watermarks. Stone carving may have natural abstract grooves but no legible words.
Avoid: embedded UI, triptych layout, fake text, strong focal objects in the right two-thirds, bright hotspots behind the future reader.
```
